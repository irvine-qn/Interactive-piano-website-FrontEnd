# How to play

The Piano page and Practice use the same keyboard. Click the keys with your mouse, use your computer keyboard, or connect a MIDI keyboard if your browser allows MIDI access.

## Keyboard zones

You never need Shift, so you can press sharps and natural notes together.

| Zone | Natural notes | Sharp notes | Move the zone |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Zone 1 | `Z X C V B N M , . /` | `S D G H J L ;` | `Left` and `Right` arrows |
| Zone 2 | `Q W E R T Y U I O P` | `2 3 5 6 7 9 0` | `Up` and `Down` arrows |

Each arrow press moves that zone by one octave. You can also use the **-** and **+** buttons. Each key shows its shortcut.

## Chords with one key

Hold a chord key, then press one note in the same zone. That note becomes the root of the chord.

| Chord | Zone 1 | Zone 2 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Major | `A` | `1` |
| Minor | `F` | `4` |
| Seventh | `K` | `8` |

For example, hold `A` and press `Z` to play a major chord in Zone 1.

## Piano page

- **Key labels:** show keyboard shortcuts with note names, or note names only.
- **Metronome:** turn it on and set the speed from 30 to 240 BPM.

## Practice a song

Open **Sheet library** and press **Practice** on any song. Use **Show sheet music** to switch between the falling notes and the score.

- **Auto Play:** the song plays by itself, and you can play along at any time.
- **Play:** notes stop at the orange line until you press the lit keys. Then the song continues.

In both modes, the song can also start when you press its first note.

The **Shortcuts** row shows the keys to press in order. Keys in the same column are pressed together, and the current column is highlighted. Arrows above the keys show when to move a zone, based on where your zones are now. Use **Hide shortcuts** to hide this row.

### Tips for Play mode

- Zones follow the song automatically, so the labels may change between chords. The arrow keys and zone buttons are locked in this mode.
- Each chord needs at most 4 notes from you. The other notes play by themselves.
- You can press the notes of a chord one after another.

### Other controls

- **Timeline:** drag to jump to another part of the song.
- **Speed:** change the tempo from 0.5x to 1.5x.
- **Restart:** go back to the beginning.
- **Heart:** like a song. You need to log in first.
